About Stellar Tweaks

Stellar Tweaks is a passion project crafted with ❤️ by @kanaochar // @kanawohosshi. What started as a fun experiment quickly evolved into a learning journey, blending creativity with technical exploration. Every design choice, and every feature was thoughtfully implemented, not just for functionality, but to deepen our understanding of development and share something useful with the community.

While building this project, we referenced various resources, tutorials, that help us to be more. We’re grateful for the knowledge shared by the developer community, and we hope Stellar Tweaks can serve as both a helpful tool and an inspiration for others who are learning.

Updates will be rolled out as frequently as needed,whether to introduce new features, refine existing ones, or simply keep things running smoothly. Your feedback and support mean the world to us. If you’ve found this project interesting, useful, or just fun to explore, thank you for being part of its journey. We appreciate every bit of encouragement, and it motivates us to keep improving!

Regards, Kanao
